Xiangqi & Cờ Úp - Play AI: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-13

바른앱 공방 ("we", the developer) publishes the Android app "Xiangqi & Cờ Úp - Play AI" ("the app"), a xiangqi (Chinese chess) and Cờ Úp (face-down xiangqi) game against a computer opponent, with endgame puzzles, classic game records and a position editor. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Summary

  • The app has no account, sign-up or login. It does not ask for your name, phone number or email address.
  • Your settings, the unfinished game, puzzle progress and the number of free undos/hints used are stored only on your phone. We run no server that receives them.
  • The computer opponent (the open-source Fairy-Stockfish engine) runs on your device and works offline.
  • Ads are shown through Google AdMob (rewarded ads only) and the optional one-time purchase "Premium" (no ads, unlimited undo/hint) is handled by Google Play Billing. Google processes some data in those services (see below).
  • We do not sell your data and we run no analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own.

Data stored on your device only

Settings (language, piece style, coordinates, level, side), the unfinished game, puzzle progress, the counters of free undos/hints used, and a cached flag telling whether Premium is owned. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes all of it. If Android backup is on, the operating system may include app data in your Google account backup; we have no access to it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The only ads are rewarded ads: when you have used up the free undos/hints of a game (or the free hints of a puzzle), the app asks whether you want to watch a short ad to use one more. There are no banner or interstitial ads. Premium owners see no ads and the ad SDK is not started for them.

When an ad is requested or shown, Google may automatically collect and process, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention: the Android advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app information, ad and app interactions, and diagnostics. Data is encrypted in transit. In regions where consent is required (EEA, UK, etc.) a Google consent form (UMP) is shown first and no ads are requested before consent; a "Privacy options" entry appears in Settings where applicable. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads). See https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Payment details are handled entirely by Google Play; the app and the developer never receive card or account details. The app only checks whether Premium is owned.

Clipboard, game records and the position editor

The endgame puzzles, the classic game records and the engine are all bundled in the app and work offline; none of them contacts a server. Game records you import and positions (FEN) you set up in the editor stay in memory while you use them and are not saved or sent anywhere. The app reads the clipboard only when you tap "Paste" and writes to it only when you tap "Copy FEN".

What we do not do

The app does not use the microphone, camera, contacts, location permission or photos. We do not share or sell personal information. Data is processed by Google only when you see an ad or make a purchase.

Children

The app is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13 (or the age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect information from children. Ads are served by Google AdMob; a parent who wants ads removed can buy Premium.

Your choices and data deletion

There is no account, so there is no account to delete. You can delete all app data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. We hold no information that identifies you. Data held by Google can be managed at https://myaccount.google.com.
